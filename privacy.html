Privacy Policy

yt-autopilot is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and in private GitHub secrets, and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
